Core Capabilities to Automate Workforce Management

Managing employees across branches, shifts, and remote locations requires a unified HR platform. HR Shastri eliminates spreadsheets and fragmented tools with an integrated suite of modules:

1. Centralized Employee Database

Maintain accurate employee profiles, emergency contacts, statutory IDs (Aadhaar, PAN, UAN), education records, and digital document vaults with role-based security.

2. Attendance & Biometric Sync

Real-time biometric device integration, GPS mobile geofencing attendance, flexible shift scheduling, night shift rules, overtime calculation, and late-mark tracking.

3. Leave Management System

Configurable leave policies (Casual, Sick, Earned, Maternity, Compensatory Off), automated leave balances, calendar view, and multi-tier manager approval workflows.

4. India Statutory Payroll

1-click payroll processing with automated Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax (PT), and Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) calculations and payslip generation.

5. Recruitment & Applicant Tracking (ATS)

Streamline hiring from job requisition to candidate screening, interview feedback loops, offer letter generation, and automated new hire onboarding.

6. Mobile Employee Self-Service (ESS)

Empower employees with dedicated iOS and Android mobile apps for punch-in, leave requests, payslip downloads, expense claim submissions, and company announcements.

Frequently Asked Questions About HR Shastri HRMS

What is an HRMS system and why does my business need one?

A Human Resource Management System (HRMS) automates end-to-end employee administration — from hiring, daily biometric attendance, and leave requests to payroll computation and tax compliance. It saves up to 80% of HR admin time, prevents payroll calculation errors, and ensures full statutory compliance.

Does HR Shastri handle Indian payroll compliance like PF, ESI, and TDS?

Yes. HR Shastri includes out-of-the-box support for Indian statutory compliance, including Employee Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), state-specific Professional Tax (PT), and TDS under both old and new income tax regimes. Generate compliance-ready reports with one click.
