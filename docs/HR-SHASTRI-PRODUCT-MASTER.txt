SECTION 1 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (Homepage / Hero)
================================================================================

HR Shastri is an all-in-one Human Resource Management System for growing
businesses. It unifies employee records, attendance, leave, Indian payroll,
recruitment, performance, training, finance tracking, and employee self-service
in one secure, role-based platform — available on web and mobile.

TAGLINE OPTIONS
---------------
• Complete HR, payroll, and people operations — built for Indian businesses.
• From hire to retire — attendance, leave, payroll, and compliance in one place.
• Empower HR. Simplify payroll. Engage every employee.

WHO IT IS FOR
-------------
• SMEs and mid-size companies with multiple branches and departments
• HR teams, finance/payroll administrators, and line managers
• Employees who need self-service (leave, payslip, WFH, claims, support tickets)
• SaaS operators managing multiple customer companies on one platform (Super Admin)

CORE ADVANTAGES
---------------
1. Single source of truth — One employee master linked to attendance, leave,
   salary slab, documents, and exit.

2. India-ready payroll — Pay slabs, allowances, loans, statutory deductions
   (EPF, ESI, PT), payslip PDF, wage and ESIC-oriented reports.

3. Flexible access control — Super Admin, Company owner, HR, custom staff roles,
   and Employee — each sees only what they should.

4. Modern workforce operations — WFH, short leave, gate pass, field visits,
   expense claims, geofencing and advanced attendance reporting.

5. Hire to retire — Recruitment, onboarding, performance, training, promotion,
   transfer, resignation, and termination.

6. Actionable reporting — Attendance, leave, timesheet, claims, birthdays,
   anniversaries, new joinees, late coming, resigned employees, and more.

7. Multi-company SaaS ready — Subscription plans, plan requests, coupons,
   orders/invoices, platform email templates.

8. Mobile workforce — API for mobile login, attendance punch, and HR workflows
   on the go.

3.1 DASHBOARD
-------------
What:    Role-aware home with HR visibility, calendars, and quick insights.
Benefit: Leaders and HR see workforce status without opening many modules.

SECTION 6 — USER ROLES: WHO CAN DO WHAT
================================================================================

The system uses TWO layers of access control:

  (1) users.type — super admin | company | hr (HR) | employee
  (2) Spatie permissions — e.g. Manage Leave, Manage Pay Slip, Manage Employee

In production, DATABASE permissions are the source of truth. Companies can
create custom staff users with custom roles (Staff → User / Role).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 SUPER ADMIN (Platform owner)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Typical menu / scope:
  • Dashboard
  • Company list (all tenant organizations)
  • Mobile menu configuration
  • Plans (subscription plans and limits)
  • Plan requests (approve/reject upgrades)
  • Coupons (if permitted)
  • Orders
  • Platform roles
  • Email templates (system-wide)
  • Impersonate / login as company or user (support feature)

Can do:
  • Onboard and manage customer companies
  • Define pricing and plan caps (users/employees)
  • Manage platform messaging templates and billing orders
  • Does NOT run day-to-day HR inside a company unless impersonating

Best for: Product owner, SaaS operator, platform support lead.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.2 COMPANY (Tenant admin / Business owner)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Identity:
  • type = company
  • Owns tenant; company data scoped under this account

Typical access (full company role — as seeded; live may differ):
  • All major HR modules when permissions granted
  • System setup: business, system, company settings
  • Email notifications, Google Calendar, leave settings
  • Full HR setup masters and letter settings
  • Subscription plan setup and invoice/order for their tenant
  • Organization-wide reports (Manage Report)
  • Finance modules when permitted

Special behavior:
  • Company wizard until branch/profile is complete
  • Subscription checks may restrict access without active plan

Can do:
  • Configure the entire organization
  • Create HR users and custom roles
  • Approve request queues
  • Run payroll generation (if permitted)
  • Full reporting and company settings

Best for: Founder, HR head, organization IT/admin owner.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.3 HR USER (type = hr / HR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Identity:
  • Staff user under a company (created_by = company id)
  • Usually assigned HR Spatie role with large permission set

Typical access (seeded HR role — live may differ):
  • Manage users (often), employees (full CRUD)
  • Payroll: salary components, payslip generation, allowance/loan/deduction
  • Attendance, leave, timesheets
  • Recruitment: jobs, applications, interviews, onboarding, career
  • Performance: indicators, appraisals, goals
  • Training: trainers, training programs
  • Promotion, transfer, resignation, termination
  • Complaints, tickets, announcements, holidays, events, policies
  • Assets (if permitted)
  • Org masters: branch, department, designation, document types, etc.

Usually cannot (unless extra permissions added):
  • Super Admin company/plan management across tenants
  • Some company-only subscription screens
  • Full system settings (depends on assigned permissions)

Can do:
  • Run daily HR operations for the company
  • Process leave, attendance, payroll, recruitment without owning the SaaS platform

Best for: HR manager, HR executive, payroll officer (permissions adjustable).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.4 CUSTOM STAFF / “USER” (Managers and specialists)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Identity:
  • Users under Staff → User with custom Spatie roles
  • Examples: Department Manager, Recruiter only, Payroll viewer

Access:
  • ONLY permissions assigned to that role

Examples:
  • Manage Leave Request — approve team leave only
  • Manage Attendance — mark or view team attendance
  • Manage Job Application — recruitment only
  • Manage Report — analytics without employee delete or settings

Marketing message:
  “Give managers exactly the access they need — nothing more.”

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.5 EMPLOYEE (type = employee)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Identity:
  • Linked to employees table row
  • Subject to web_login, app_login, employee_exit, login_status

Typical menu:
  • Dashboard
  • Employee details (own profile)
  • My payslip
  • Attendance & leaves: timesheet, leave, my leave balance
  • Apply WFH, short leave, gate pass
  • My visit and my claim
  • Announcements, holidays, events (per permissions)
  • Company policy (view)
  • Complaints and tickets (if permitted)
  • Promotion / transfer (view if permitted)
  • Training list
  • Resignation flows where allowed

Usually cannot:
  • View other employees’ salary (unless misconfigured)
  • Generate payroll or change company settings
  • Manage staff users or full HR reports

Best for: Every employee — self-service and transparency.

SECTION 7 — PERMISSION MODEL (For technical buyers)
================================================================================

• Permissions use action names such as:
  Manage Employee, Create Pay Slip, Manage Leave Request,
  Manage Gate Pass Request, Approved Claims, Manage Company Settings, etc.

• Navigation hides unauthorized menu items (@can / Gate::check).

• Company administrators define custom roles and assign them to staff users.

• Implementation note: Always align marketing claims with permissions enabled
  in the customer’s live database.
